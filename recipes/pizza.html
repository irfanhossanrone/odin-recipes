Pizza

Description

Pizza is a beloved Italian dish consisting of a flattened round base of dough topped with sauce, cheese, and various ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced pepperoni
  • 1/4 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup sliced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup sliced onions

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Add your desired toppings.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  8. Slice and serve hot.
