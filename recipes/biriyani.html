Biriyani

Description

Biriyani is a popular rice dish that is enjoyed across many cultures. It is typically made with aromatic spices, herbs, and your choice of protein.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish

Steps

  1. Rinse the basmati rice and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add the sliced onions. Cook until golden brown.
  3. Add the minced garlic and ginger. Cook for a minute.
  4. Add the cumin seeds, bay leaves, turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder. Stir for a minute.
  5. Add the rinsed rice and cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly.
  6. Add water and salt to taste. Bring to a boil.
  7. Reduce heat and cover. Simmer for 15-20 minutes or until the rice is cooked.
  8. Mix in the yogurt and lemon juice. Garnish with fresh cilantro.
