Pasta

Description

Pasta is a versatile Italian dish made from durum wheat semolina and water. It can be served with a variety of sauces and toppings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil for garnish

Steps

  1. In a bowl, combine the flour, salt, and pepper.
  2. Make a well in the center and crack the eggs into it.
  3. Mix the ingredients together to form a dough.
  4. Knead the dough for 10 minutes until smooth.
  5. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
  6. Roll out the dough and cut into desired shapes.
  7. Cook the pasta in boiling water until al done.
  8. Drain the pasta and toss with olive oil.
  9. Serve with tomato sauce and Parmesan cheese. Garnish with fresh basil.
